3 Strange Farm Candies You Forgot

True, odd moments from history, told short and checked against at least two sources.

The story

Before candy corn ruled Halloween, kids were snacking on candy turnips. Around the 1880s, candy makers molded mellowcreme into farm shapes and sold to farm and country kids all year round. One, turnips. Two, chestnuts. Three, clover leaves. Back then, about half of America's workers were on farms. So what really happened? Candy corn stuck around, but the candy turnips not so much.
