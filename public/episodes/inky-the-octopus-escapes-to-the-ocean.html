Inky the Octopus Escapes to the Ocean

True, odd moments from history, told short and checked against at least two sources.

The story

(18) An octopus broke out of his tank, crossed the floor, and went down a drain to the ocean. (16) Meet Inky, a common New Zealand octopus at the National Aquarium in Napier. (16) Early in twenty sixteen, staff say a lid was left slightly open after maintenance. (24) Inky squeezed through a small gap, slid across the floor, and found a drainpipe about six inches wide that led to the sea.

(18) Staff followed wet octopus tracks. His tank-mate stayed put. Inky was never seen again. (18) Octopuses can squeeze through anything bigger than their beak, the only hard part of the body. (14) What really happened? A curious octopus took the shortcut home. Staff hope he's doing fine.
