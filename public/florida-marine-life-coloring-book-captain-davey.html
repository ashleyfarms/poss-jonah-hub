Use it for an ocean unit or a beach trip

Ocean nature study: do one critter per lesson. Read the facts, color the page, then find a video or aquarium visit to see the animal move. Kids learn that a dolphin has its own whistle like a name, that an octopus has three hearts and blue blood, that spiny lobsters march in a line like a parade, and that loggerhead hatchlings need dark beaches to find the sea.

Creation and care: faith-based homeschools can open with Genesis 1:20–21 or Psalm 104:24–25 and talk about caring for the creatures God made. The book's own fact about sea turtles needing dark beaches is a great place to start.

Florida vacation: pack it with the beach bag and use the “My Sea Critter Notes” pages to log what you spot.
