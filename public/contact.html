Contact Poss Jonah

Poss Jonah is run by Albert Jones, a one-man shop. We read every message, and we'd love to hear from you.

How to reach us

The easiest way is the contact form on our parent site, Help-Pal: help-pal-apps.com/contact. Messages go straight to Albert.

You can also find Poss Jonah on X and TikTok, where comments are welcome.

Good reasons to write

  • Corrections. Every story we tell is checked against at least two sources, but mistakes happen. Tell us which video or article, what we got wrong, and where you read otherwise, and we'll fix it and note the change.
  • Story ideas. Got a local legend, an odd bit of history or a strange animal you think we should cover? Send it in.
  • Best-of nominations. Know the best sweet tea, BBQ or fried chicken in your town? Nominate it on the rankings, or write and tell us about it.
  • Books. Questions about our coloring books and picture books are welcome; see the Books page.
  • Photo credits. If you own a picture we used and the credit is wrong, let us know and we'll correct it right away.

What to expect

We usually answer within a few days. We don't sell or share your email address; see our Privacy Policy.
