Questions families ask

Are these books a complete curriculum?

No. They're affordable workbooks and nature books that work as a homeschool curriculum supplement, either on their own or next to any program you already use. None of them claim to follow a particular standard.

Are they Christian?

The math, states and nature books teach their subjects without Bible lessons. The Night Before Christmas includes the nativity story from Scripture, and Renewed Every Morning is a Bible verse devotional. All of them are made by Poss Jonah, and they fit faith-based homeschools well.
