What each day's page holds

  • One verse, printed in full from the World English Bible, a public-domain modern English translation. No verse repeats anywhere in the book.
  • A short reflection, usually three or four sentences. It's honest about tired mornings and full calendars, and it points back to the verse.
  • A one-line prayer (“A PRAYER”) or a short phrase to hold onto (“CARRY THIS TODAY”).

A day takes about two minutes. There's a bonus page for February 29 after February 28, so the book works in leap years too.

Who this women's devotional is for

The busy mom, including the homeschool mom who teaches all day and wants a moment in the Word before the kids wake up. The woman coming back to faith, who wants something gentle and short. The faithful Bible reader who wants one verse to think about between longer readings. And anyone who gives gifts to women, because a dated book can be started on any day, so it works as a gift in December, on a birthday, or on Mother's Day.

Ways to use it

  • Morning quiet time: keep it by the coffee pot and read the day's page first.
  • Women's Bible study or small group: everyone reads the same date, then shares what stood out.
  • Journaling: copy the verse by hand and write one sentence back to God.
  • With older kids: read the verse aloud at breakfast; the reflections are written for grown-ups, but the verses fit the whole family.
  • Online companion: as the book notes, today's verse also appears at verse.help-pal-apps.com.

Questions families ask

Do I have to start on January 1?

No. Each page is dated, and the book is meant to be opened to today's date in any year. Start the day it arrives.

Which Bible translation does it use?

The World English Bible, a public-domain modern English translation. Each verse shows its reference.

Is it a reading plan through the whole Bible?

No. It's one short verse a day with a reflection, about two minutes. It works well next to a longer reading plan.

Is there a page for February 29?

Yes, a bonus page follows February 28. In other years, just turn past it.
