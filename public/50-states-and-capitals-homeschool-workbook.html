A simple homeschool plan for the 50 states

Two states a week takes one school year, which is an easy geography rhythm. One state a day takes about ten weeks, a good fit for a summer or a focused unit.

For each state: (1) find it on the big U.S. map, (2) color the outline and write the capital, (3) read the facts page together, and (4) mark it on the checklist. Some ideas to go further:

  • Bird and flower notebook: look up a photo of the state bird and draw it.
  • Capitals drill: quiz each other at lunch using the A–Z index.
  • Family connections: start with states where relatives live, or states you've visited.
  • Nature tie-in: pair the Southern states with Birds from Around the World: The American South, and Florida with the Florida marine life book.
