Christian Books for Kids and Families

Faith-based books from Poss Jonah. There's a Christian Christmas picture book that tells the nativity story from the Bible, a 365-day Bible verse devotional for women, and gentle moral stories from Possum Holler. They're made for family devotions, homeschool Bible time, and Sunday school.

A Christian Christmas book for children: the nativity story

The Night Before Christmas, with Poss Jonah and the Possum Holler Friends starts with Moore's classic poem. Then it turns to the reason for the season. Poss gathers the Holler kids by the fire and tells the story of Jesus' birth across 34 painted pages, from the promises in Isaiah and Micah through Gabriel, Mary and Joseph, the manger, the shepherds, Simeon and Anna, and the wise men. Each page has a Scripture reference and a kid-sized Why it matters.

A daily devotional for women

Renewed Every Morning gives every date of the year one Bible verse, a short reflection, and a one-line prayer or a phrase to carry. It takes about two minutes. Each month has a theme, from “New mercies” in January to “Light, waiting, and joy” in December. It's a thoughtful Christian gift for moms, grandmothers, teachers and friends.

Questions families ask

Which Poss Jonah books are Christian books?

The Night Before Christmas includes a 34-page nativity story told from the Bible, and Renewed Every Morning is a daily Bible verse devotional for women. The Possum Holler stories are gentle moral tales that fit Christian homes, with Bible pairings suggested on their page.

Which Bible translation do they quote?

The World English Bible, a public-domain modern English translation.

Are these good for Sunday school?

The nativity pages work well for a small group. Each page has a big painting and a Scripture reference to open alongside.
