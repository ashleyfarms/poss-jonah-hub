A homeschool nature study unit in one book

Charlotte Mason-style and nature-loving homeschools can build a simple bird unit around it. Do one bird a week and you have a 15-week nature study:

  1. Read the color page together and talk about the male and female.
  2. Color the matching page while looking at the color picture.
  3. Watch for that bird at the feeder or on a walk that week.
  4. Draw and date what you saw on a “My Bird Notes” page.

Kids learn fun facts along the way. A mockingbird can copy about 200 songs, baby wood ducks jump out of the nest the day after they hatch, and a bobwhite says its own name.
