Using it in your homeschool

As a main workbook for a light first-grade math year: do one page a day, four days a week, and you'll finish in about 11 weeks. On the fifth day, play a math game or redo a page that was hard.

Alongside your curriculum: use it for extra practice when your main program gets to addition and subtraction within 20, or for summer review so skills don't slip.

Tips that help first graders: keep ten real objects in a cup for the ten-frame and make-10 pages. Read story problems aloud the first few times. Let your child check their own work with the answer key, because finding a mistake is part of learning.

Getting ready? If your child is still working on writing numbers to 20, start with Math with Poss: Counting 1–20.
